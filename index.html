Our ambulance services

Whatever the situation, we send the right vehicle with trained staff.

Emergency ambulance

Quick response for accidents, heart attacks and sudden illness, with basic life support on board.

ICU & ventilator ambulance

Critical-care transport with oxygen, monitors and ventilator support for serious patients.

Patient transfer

Comfortable, planned transport between homes, hospitals and cities, including discharge and dialysis trips.

Freezer box service

An ambulance and staff on site for weddings, sports, gatherings and other public events.

Oxygen cylinders and trained attendants available for patients who need support during travel.
